What we actually use in Zoho, and what a replacement must not miss

A walkthrough of Ampera's live Zoho Projects, People and Payroll portals, our Projects data pulled read-only through the API, Zoho's pricing pages, and about 90 community and review sources. The goal is to check the baseline idea (RBAC, projects, members, timesheets, leave, payslips) and list the small things it leaves out.

Projects: Enterprise plan16 projectsviewed as: Employee profile30 screenshots~90 sources
Headline findings
  1. Zoho Projects is used as a timesheet charge-code system.Every project has 0 milestones and 0 issues, and nobody uses dependencies, comments or attachments. What matters is the time logs: billable vs non-billable, approval status, and an ATPL-46 Organizational Tasks project holding 16 standing codes (Bench, Training, Proposal ≈ 1,609 h logged). Client and SOW codes are faked with tags like SOW-11101.
  2. The three apps disagree about the same day.On Tue 10 Nov, Projects shows 8 h logged. Zoho People marks the same day "Absent", Attendance says "Present: 0 days", and Leave Summary says "Absent: 0". People also has its own Time Tracker, and it's empty.
  3. Approval details exist but are hidden.A leave request's approver, level (1/1) and "Acted in 4 days" appear only behind a View button. The list shows a green tick and nothing else, and a 4-day wait triggered no escalation. In Projects, one approval produced 5 identical notifications with no hours or dates.
  4. Notifications are noisy and coarse.99+ unread in Projects. The only setting is on or off per project. There are no per-event choices and no digest.
  5. The menu shows things you can't open.Employees see a Timesheets item that leads to "Permission Denied". Home didn't finish loading after more than 11 seconds.

Gated features to know about

  • Projects: multi-user / multi-project timesheets are Ultimate only.
  • Projects: custom fields and custom roles need Enterprise.
  • Projects: client users are a paid add-on on every plan.
  • People: attendance and timesheets start at Professional.
  • Payroll India: leave and attendance plus workflow rules are Premium only. No audit trail on Free.

Bundles

  • Zoho One costs about 3x the standalone stack for these three apps (₹31,250 vs ₹10,400 per month at 25 users).
  • People Plus bundles People + Payroll but not Projects.
  • Zoho raised People Plus prices in July 2026 (Premium $4 → $8).

Suggested scope

A starting point for the build brainstorm

Must

  • Org, users, five plain roles scoped by reporting line and project
  • Clients → SOW → projects → tasks, with keys
  • Org-level charge codes (Bench, Training, Proposal…)
  • Week timesheet: billable flag, notes, approval with reason and approver
  • Leave types, balances, apply/approve with a visible timeline
  • One holiday calendar and work week shared by leave and timesheets
  • Approved leave fills the timesheet automatically
  • Payslip view and download
  • One approvals inbox and in-app notifications with a digest
  • Activity history on every record

Should

  • Reminders for missing timesheet days (skipping leave and holidays)
  • Delegation and escalation when approvals stall
  • Utilization, timesheet compliance and leave reports, CSV export
  • Investment declarations with an admin window

Later

  • Gantt, milestones, issue tracker, blueprints (unused today)
  • Attendance check-in and geo-fencing
  • Full payroll engine with statutory calculation

Out of scope: mobile app. The build is web only.

Method and limits
  • Read-only throughout. The only clicks were navigation, view switches and opening forms, which were then cancelled. Nothing was submitted, approved or changed.
  • Seen through an Employee profile, so admin settings, billing, and the Timesheets approval module were out of reach. People and Payroll plan tiers are unknown.
  • Screenshots include colleagues' names, so check before sharing this page outside the team.
